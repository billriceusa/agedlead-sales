Quick Tips From the Trenches

Exclusive insights — only in the newsletter

The 3-Touch Summer Test

Test your aged leads' summer responsiveness with this sequence: initial call, text 2 hours later, email same day. If you don't get a response in 72 hours, flag them for September reactivation. Summer prospects who don't respond to this sequence are typically vacation-mode until fall.

ROI Reset Every 90 Days

Recalculate your aged lead ROI quarterly, not annually. Market conditions change fast in 2026. Use this simple check: if your customer acquisition cost from aged leads is more than 40% of average deal value, you're either buying wrong or working them wrong.

The Scheduling Link Consent Hack

Add this exact language to your scheduling link booking form: 'By scheduling this appointment, I consent to receive calls and texts about my request at the number provided.' One sentence turns a cold aged lead into documented consent for follow-up.
