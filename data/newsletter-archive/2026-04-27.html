The Economics of Your First Hire

If you're closing 15+ deals per month from aged leads, you can afford a $40K/year specialist. Here's why: at $2,000 average commission, that's $30K monthly revenue. A specialist handling follow-up and nurture can add 3-5 additional closings per month ($6K-$10K), easily covering their salary plus overhead.

Lead Distribution: Round-Robin vs Cherry-Pick

Round-robin beats cherry-picking in 90% of situations because it prevents lead hoarding and creates accountability. Exception: if you have one superstar closing 25%+ while others struggle at 8%, let them cherry-pick premium leads until you level up the team. Otherwise, equal distribution forces everyone to improve.

The One Metric That Reveals Scale-Readiness

Track your "follow-up abandonment rate" — leads that get 1-2 touches then die. If you're abandoning more than 40% of purchased leads due to time constraints, you're ready to scale. That abandoned inventory represents pure profit opportunity for a systematic team approach.
