Quick Tips From the Trenches

Exclusive insights — only in the newsletter

Tag leads by age tier before you dial

Split your list into 30-, 60-, and 90+ day buckets before your first call session. A 30-day lead still remembers filling out the form, so you can reference it directly. A 90-day lead needs a softer, 'checking back in' opener because the original request has faded from memory. Working all three tiers with the same script is why contact rates drop off — the pitch doesn't match where the prospect's head is at.

The reactivation text that outperforms a cold call

Before you burn a dial on an aged lead you haven't reached in a week, send this text first: 'Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] with [Company] — you'd requested info on [topic] a while back. Still an open question for you, or should I close out your file?' The word 'close out your file' creates urgency without pressure, and it gives you a natural reply to work with instead of a cold pickup.

Stack your calls on Wednesday and Thursday afternoons

Multiple published call-timing studies (including the widely cited InsideSales.com research on contact rates) point to Wednesday and Thursday between 4-6pm local time as the highest-percentage windows for reaching a live prospect by phone. If your dialer lets you weight call blocks, front-load your aged lead sessions into those windows and use Monday mornings for admin and list prep instead.

Industry Insight

The consent rule fight isn't over — and it affects how you buy

In January 2025, the 11th Circuit Court of Appeals vacated the FCC's one-to-one consent rule that would have required separate, itemized consent for every marketer contacting a lead. That ruling is still shaping how lead generators structure consent language in 2026, and it's a reminder that the compliance ground under purchased data keeps shifting. Whatever list you're working, know exactly what consent language was captured at the point of collection — don't assume.
