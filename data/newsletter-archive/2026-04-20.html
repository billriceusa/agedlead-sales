The DNC Scrub Step Most Agents Skip

Before calling aged leads, scrub against the National DNC Registry AND your internal DNC list. But here's what most miss: also scrub against your company's previous campaign DNC requests from the last 12 months. Those prospects who said 'remove me' on your insurance campaign shouldn't get your mortgage calls either. Missing this costs $500+ per violation under TCPA.

Manual Dial vs Power Dial: The Compliance Difference

Power dialers that connect calls before an agent is available create 'abandoned calls'—hanging up on prospects when no agent picks up. FCC considers 3%+ abandonment rate a violation. Solution: use 'preview dial' mode where agents see the lead first, then manually initiate the call. Slower but eliminates abandonment risk entirely.

Your 5-Record Compliance Checklist

Before every campaign: (1) Verify lead consent date is within your industry's limits, (2) Confirm your caller ID matches your registered business name, (3) Check that your scripts include opt-out language, (4) Set your dialer to manual/preview mode, (5) Load your suppression lists (DNC, internal, previous opt-outs). Takes 10 minutes, saves thousands in fines.

Industry Insight

FTC Ramping Up Lead Gen Enforcement

The FTC's recent Operation Lead Rush resulted in over $100M in penalties against lead generation companies for deceptive practices. This increased scrutiny is filtering down to lead buyers, with more states requiring agents to maintain consent records and call recordings for compliance audits.
