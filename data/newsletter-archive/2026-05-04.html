Free CRM vs Paid: The Real Cost Breakdown

HubSpot Free looks attractive until you hit 1,000 contacts and need $45/month per user. For aged lead volume, you're better off with LeadConnector at $97/month (unlimited contacts) or even Zoho CRM at $14/month per user. The "free" tier math breaks at exactly 847 aged leads per month — do the calculation before you commit.

Triple Your Daily Contact Volume with This Dialer Setup

Set your predictive dialer to 3.2x ratio during peak hours (10am-2pm, 5pm-7pm), drop to 1.8x during off-peak. Enable voicemail drop with a 12-second message max. Most importantly: pre-load tomorrow's call list at 4pm today so your dialer starts immediately at 9am instead of spending 20 minutes importing records.

3 Chrome Extensions Every Lead Caller Needs

TrueCaller for instant caller ID verification ($4/month). Loom for quick video follow-ups to warm leads (free tier works). TextExpander for instant objection responses and appointment confirmations ($3.33/month). Install all three and you'll save 45 minutes per day on repetitive tasks while improving your contact quality.
