Quick Tips From the Trenches

Exclusive insights — only in the newsletter

Build Your AEP Pipeline Now

Start buying aged Medicare leads 60-90 days before AEP to build relationships early. Focus on leads aged 30-90 days old — fresh enough for decent contact rates, old enough to be affordable. Set aside $2,000-5,000 now to secure inventory before September pricing spikes hit.

The SEP Strategy Most Agents Ignore

Special Enrollment Periods run year-round, but most agents only work AEP. Build a systematic SEP pipeline by targeting aged leads from people who moved, lost coverage, or had qualifying life events. Use the question: 'Have you had any changes in your living situation or health coverage in the past 60 days?' to identify SEP eligibility.

Medicare Lead Aging Sweet Spot

Medicare leads perform best between 14-45 days old. Under 14 days, you're competing with every agent who bought the same lead. Over 45 days, the urgency drops significantly. Buy in this window and work them systematically with 3 touches per week for maximum ROI.
