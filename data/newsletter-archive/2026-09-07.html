Quick Tips From the Trenches

Exclusive insights — only in the newsletter

Know Your CPL Before You Ever Click Buy

Your real ceiling on cost-per-lead is math, not a feeling: Max CPL = (average commission × close rate) minus the margin you need to stay profitable. Say your average commission is $600 and you close 8% of the leads you actually work — your breakeven CPL is $48. Back that off another 30-40% for real margin and you've got your true number. The Know Your CPL calculator on workagedleads.com runs this for you instantly — plug in your commission and close rate, get your ceiling.

Reverse-Engineer Revenue Into a Daily Dial Count

Work backward: revenue goal ÷ average commission = deals needed. Deals needed ÷ your close rate = appointments needed. Appointments ÷ your show/contact rate = dials needed per month, then divide by working days. A hypothetical: $12,000 goal ÷ $600 average commission = 20 deals. At a 10% close rate, that's 200 appointments. At a 20% contact-to-appointment rate, that's 1,000 dials a month, or roughly 45 a day. The Pipeline Calculator on the site does this chain automatically — feed it your numbers instead of guessing.

Check the Lead Price Index Before You Assume You're Overpaying

The Lead Price Index tracks what the market is actually paying right now, broken out by vertical and lead age — fresh, 30-day, 90-day, and beyond. It's the fastest way to tell if a quote you got is in line with the market or out of it, without guessing. Pair it with the glossary if terms like 'shared' vs 'exclusive' or 'aged' vs 'semi-fresh' still trip you up, and the provider comparison pages when you're deciding who to actually buy from.

Industry Insight

Fresh leads are expensive by design — that's the whole aged-lead thesis

WordStream's annual Google Ads benchmark report has shown for years that cost-per-lead in insurance, legal, and financial services regularly runs into the triple digits when generated through paid search — before you've spoken to a single prospect. That's the acquisition cost baked into every fresh lead you buy. Aged leads carry the same underlying consumer data at a fraction of that acquisition cost, which is exactly why a consistent, sustainable aged-lead buy consistently outperforms sporadic expensive buying over a full year.
