Quick Tips From the Trenches

Exclusive insights — only in the newsletter

The 50-Dial Final Expense Minimum

Agents who consistently hit 50+ dials per day in final expense average 3.2x more policies written than those who don't. This isn't about skill — it's pure math. Final expense aged leads typically convert at 0.8-1.2%, so 50 dials gets you into statistical significance territory where your close rate stabilizes. Below 50, you're still in random outcome land.

Contact Rate Trumps Close Rate

A 15% contact rate with a 10% close rate beats a 8% contact rate with a 20% close rate every time. Focus your coaching on getting more people on the phone first. For aged final expense leads, anything above 12% contact rate indicates good list quality. Below 8% usually means the leads are too old or poorly sourced.

The 3-Touch Rule for Final Expense Callbacks

If someone asks you to call back in final expense, they're 4x more likely to buy than a cold dial — but only if you actually call back. Set a maximum of 3 callback attempts spread over 5-7 days. After that, the urgency that drove their initial interest has typically faded and conversion rates drop to baseline levels.
