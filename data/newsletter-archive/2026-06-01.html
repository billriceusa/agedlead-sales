Quick Tips From the Trenches

Exclusive insights — only in the newsletter

The 90-day attribution window

Track every aged lead sale back 90 days, not just to last contact. Say you buy a 180-day aged lead in January, make contact in February, and close in March. Standard CRM attribution credits February's activity. Reality: January's lead purchase enabled March's commission. Use a 90-day lookback window for accurate ROI calculations.

Month-3 performance multiplier

Your third month working aged leads should show 2-3x better performance than month one. If you're not seeing this lift, you're either burning through leads too fast or not building proper nurture sequences. Track your month-over-month contact rates and appointment-to-close ratios as leading indicators.

The lead age sweet spot formula

For every 30 days of lead age, expect contact rates to drop 8-12%. But pricing drops 15-25%. This creates arbitrage windows. Most profitable zone: 90-180 day aged leads where contact rates are still viable (40-60%) but pricing is heavily discounted. Test this range across your verticals.

Industry Insight

Multi-touch attribution gains traction

More aged lead buyers are adopting multi-touch attribution models that track customer journeys across months instead of crediting just the last interaction. This shift reveals aged leads often contribute to sales cycles that traditional attribution models miss entirely, improving ROI calculations by 20-40%.
