**Collected 2026-10-07, re-verified 2026-10-12.** Every figure below is quoted
from a live public page. Nothing here is estimated, averaged or normalized.

This file is the evidence base, not the published index. It exists because the
collection found that a blended two-provider benchmark **cannot honestly be
built for Q4** — see §4 — and that conclusion needs its working shown.

## 4. Why a blended Q4 benchmark cannot be built

The schema keys a benchmark on **vertical × age bracket × exclusivity × lead
type**, and defines exclusivity by buyer count:

- `exclusive` = sold to 1 buyer
- `semi-exclusive` = sold to 2-3 buyers
- `shared` = sold to 4+ buyers

Our two publishing providers sit in **different buckets**:

| Provider | Stated exclusivity | Schema bucket |
|---|---|---|
| Aged Lead Store | "shared Internet leads" | `shared` |
| Badass Insurance Leads | "exclusive to the client, never resold by us" | `exclusive` |

So in every vertical where both publish — mortgage refi, life/FEX, health, IUL —
each bucket still has **exactly one provider**. `isTrustworthyBenchmark` requires
two, and the public methodology promises "we only publish a benchmark backed by
more than one provider."

**Counting the sheet raised coverage from one provider to two. It did not
produce a single comparable pair.** Blending them would mean averaging a
shared lead against an exclusive one and calling it a market rate — which is
exactly the kind of number the index exists to replace.

Two further obstacles even if exclusivity matched:

1. **Age brackets do not align.** ALS uses 3-30 / 31-85 / 86-365; Badass uses
   15-90 / 90-365; the schema offers 1-7 / 8-30 / 31-85 / 86-180 / 181-365.
   No clean mapping exists for either provider.
2. **Volume tiers barely overlap.** Badass wholesale starts at 1,001 (3,000 for
   mortgage). ALS spans 1-249 upward. Only three tiers overlap, and a buyer of
   200 leads is in a different market from a buyer of 10,000.

## 6. Freshness and trust flags

- **Brokers Data** still runs live pages for HARP and "HARP 2.0", programs that
  ended December 2018.
- **Lead Heroes** marketing page carries a 2022 copyright and a 2022-02-25
  modified date; prices survive only on the order page (modified 2026-08-10).
  That order page also contradicts itself: English "480+ hours ($12 per Hour)"
  is $11,520 (= 960 hours) while Spanish at the same label is $5,760 (= 480).
- **Badass** Spanish Medicare product page body describes "aged Spanish
  **mortgage** insurance leads" — copy-paste error.
- **Aged Lead Store** publishes three different phone numbers across properties.
- **Cross-provider disagreement, reported as disagreement and NOT as law:**
  Brokers Data asserts "TCPA opt in for internet leads is 90 days... purge aged
  leads that are older than 90 days." Both ALS (86-365, 86-500) and Badass
  (90-365, 90-500) sell well past that. This is a vendor marketing claim with no
  statutory citation. Per the primary-source rule it must not be repeated as
  law, but the disagreement itself is real and worth reporting.
