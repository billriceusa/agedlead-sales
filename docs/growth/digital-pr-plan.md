# Digital-PR / Link-Building Plan — agedleadsales.com

**Created:** 2026-06-05 · **Owner:** Bill Rice · **Goal:** earn genuine, relevant
backlinks to lift Domain Rating (currently 2.5) and rankings.

## Why this is the lever

The site is fully built and indexed (~30+ pages live in Google). On-page work is
essentially done. The toxic-link liability is disavowed (see
[`../../data/backlink-audit/`](../../data/backlink-audit/)). What's left — the
*only* thing that moves a DR-2.5 site — is real authority. This plan is the
offense to the disavow's defense.

**Reality check:** we need *quality, not quantity*. After cleaning up 146 spam
domains, the last thing we want is more low-quality links. Target a handful of
genuinely relevant, real-traffic domains. 5–10 good links beats 100 junk ones.

## Our linkable assets (lead with these)

These already exist and are what journalists/editors actually link to — original
data and tools, not sales pages.

1. **Aged Lead Industry Statistics (2026)** — `/blog/aged-lead-industry-statistics`
   — original pricing benchmarks across 14 verticals, contact rates, provider
   landscape. **This is the #1 pitch asset** (data pages earn the most links).
2. **Lead Price Index** — `/price-index` — monthly pricing benchmarks. Update =
   a recurring news hook ("Q3 2026 aged-lead prices").
3. **Free calculators** — `/calculators/*` (ROI, CPL, pipeline). Embeddable; good
   for "tools roundup" link placements.
4. **The Economics of Aged Leads** — `/blog/economics-of-aged-leads` — strong
   contrarian-data narrative ("why $2 beats $50").
5. **Bill Rice's expertise** — 30+ yrs, millions of leads worked. The credibility
   that makes expert-source pitches land.

**Cadence:** scan the daily digests, answer only queries where Bill has a genuine,
specific, data-backed take (insurance/mortgage/lead-gen/sales). 2–3 quality
answers/week. I can draft responses from the statistics page + Bill's experience.

## Channel 3 — Owned network + relationships (controlled, do carefully)

- **Sister properties:** billrice.com already links (6 dofollow). A *single*,
  contextual, editorial link from howtoworkleads.com and other BRSG sites is fine
  — but keep it natural and minimal. Do NOT build a link wheel; Google sees
  footprint patterns, and we just cleaned one up.
- **Podcast guesting:** Bill as a guest on insurance/mortgage sales podcasts →
  show-notes link + authority. High ROI, leverages existing relationships.
- **Vendor/partner pages:** AgedLeadStore.com (the affiliate partner) — a
  "featured in / as reviewed by" link is mutually beneficial and legitimate.

## Channel 4 — Resource-list / tool placements

Find existing "best sales calculators," "lead-gen resources," "insurance agent
tools" roundups and pitch the free calculators for inclusion. Low effort, real
relevance.

## 30-day starter sprint

1. **Week 1:** register Bill on SOS, Qwoted, Featured. I draft a reusable bio +
   3 canned data-answers from the statistics page.
2. **Week 1–2:** I draft 1 bylined article (vendor-neutral lead-economics piece);
   Bill reviews + we pitch Insurance Journal / NMP.
3. **Week 2:** 1 contextual link each from 2 owned properties (natural placement).
4. **Weeks 2–4:** answer 2–3 source queries/week; pitch the calculators to 5
   resource roundups.
5. **Ongoing:** every `/price-index` monthly update = a fresh data hook to pitch.

**Target:** 5–10 genuine referring domains in 90 days. Track new refdomains in the
monthly Ahrefs re-audit (same run as the disavow refresh).

recovering *from* a spam profile, not adding to one.
