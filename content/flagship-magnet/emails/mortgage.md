## Email 3 — Day 4 (outreach + scripts)

**Subject:** The 14-day cadence for aged mortgage leads

**Preheader:** Most LOs quit after 2 calls. Winners run 14 days of multi-channel touches.

---

{{firstName}},

Today's the meat of the playbook: the 14-day opener cadence.

Quick truth: single-channel outreach on aged mortgage leads is malpractice. The prospect you can't reach by phone today might answer a text at 6 PM. The one who ignores your voicemail might open an email two days later. **Multi-channel over 14 days beats 14 calls on one channel, every time.**

**The cadence at a glance:**

- **Day 1:** Call (AM) + voicemail + SMS
- **Day 2:** Email
- **Day 3:** Call (PM) + SMS
- **Day 5:** Call (evening) + Email
- **Day 7:** Call (AM, new day of week) + break-up SMS
- **Day 10:** Break-up email
- **Day 14:** Final call — then move to nurture

14 days. 5 calls. 3 texts. 3 emails.

**The opener that converts:**

> "Hey [Name], this is Bill with [Company]. A couple months back you looked at refinancing — rates have moved since then, and I wanted to see if you're still thinking about it or if you got it handled."

Shut up. Wait for the answer.

If they say "still looking": go straight to diagnosis — "What rate would make it worth moving?"

If they say "got it handled": probe — "Nice, who'd you end up with?" Half the time, "handled" means "I didn't do anything." The question re-opens the conversation without making them defensive.

**Your move this week:**

1. Open Worksheet 5 in the workbook — customize this script to your name, company, and vertical specifics.
2. Say it out loud 5 times.
3. Put the 14-day cadence into your CRM or sheet (Worksheet 6 has the template).

**Rate-move scripts only work with current data.** Have Freddie Mac PMMS open during every dial. When a prospect asks "how much have rates moved," you need to answer in seconds, not minutes.

## Email 4 — Day 7 (infinity nurture)

**Subject:** The 85% that pays your rent

**Preheader:** Most LOs abandon non-closers after Day 14. Here's what to do instead.

---

{{firstName}},

After 14 days of your opener cadence, 85–90% of your aged mortgage leads will not have closed. This is where most LOs make their biggest mistake: they **abandon the 85%**.

They buy a new list. Run the cadence. Abandon again. Rinse, repeat.

The math of that is brutal. If you buy 1,000 aged mortgage leads per month and abandon 90% after Day 14, you accumulate zero long-term asset. After 12 months you have nothing but a dialer log.

If instead you nurture the 90%, after 12 months you have a database of 10,800 prospects — all of whom raised their hand for a mortgage product at some point. **A 1% annual close rate on that database is 108 funded loans at near-zero marginal cost.**

That's your second business. And for most mortgage operators, it eventually produces more revenue than the opener cadence.

**The three-bucket system:**

- **Warm** — responded to opener but didn't close. 2-week cadence for 3 months.
- **Cold** — didn't respond, didn't opt out. Touches at Month 1, 3, 6, 9, 12.
- **Dormant** — 12+ months without response. Monthly newsletter only.

**For mortgage specifically:** your best nurture trigger is a meaningful rate move. When 30-year fixed drops 0.25%+ in a month, reach out to every Cold and Dormant lead. Keep it factual: "Rates dropped meaningfully since you shopped. Worth revisiting your numbers?" This is your highest-converting nurture touch, by a wide margin.

## Email 5 — Day 10 (compliance + final CTA)

**Subject:** Your playbook is only as good as your compliance

**Preheader:** DNC, TCPA, state DOI — the rules that keep your operation alive.

---

{{firstName}},

Last email of the course. I want to close with the least-fun but most important topic: compliance.

Here's the truth: every operator who skips DNC scrubbing, ignores state mini-TCPAs, or runs autodialed SMS on inherited consent is going to collect a demand letter sooner or later. You get to eat their market share — on the one condition that you stay clean.

**The mortgage compliance non-negotiables:**

1. **DNC scrub every list, every time.** Federal + any state DNC that applies. Cost: $0.005–$0.02 per number. Skip it and the downside is $500–$1,500+ in statutory damages per call. FL, OK, WA, MD layer stricter state frameworks on top — if you're calling into those states, scrub harder.

2. **NMLS disclosure** on every outbound call. "Bill Rice, NMLS [number], with [Company]." Write it into every script.

3. **Run Mode A on inherited aged-lead consent.** Manual dial, plain-text email. Save SMS and automation for prospects who have given you **fresh, direct consent** (the Fresh-Consent Ladder in Part IV of the playbook). The FCC's proposed 1-to-1 consent rule was vacated (Jan 2025) and repealed (Aug 2025), but the safest posture is still to capture PEWC yourself rather than rely on inherited records.

4. **Call time windows.** 8 AM–9 PM federal, but **8 PM cap in FL, OK, WA, MD** (and several others). Know your prospect's time zone.

5. **State call caps.** Florida FTSA and Oklahoma OTSA limit you to 3 calls/texts per day on the same subject. Exceeding that is a statutory violation.

6. **UDAAP hygiene.** No "rates are about to explode" language on quiet days. No pressure tactics on elderly prospects. Plain English, accurate numbers, no hype.

**When you have a real compliance question, the firm I trust is [Henson Legal](https://www.henson-legal.com/).** They're who I call. Budget $300–$500 for an initial consult before you run your first aged campaign — it's the best compliance investment you can make.

Compliance isn't a tax. It's the floor that lets you build higher than every operator who chose shortcuts.
