# The Aged Lead Operator's Workbook

*Companion to The Aged Lead Operator's System. Do these 10 worksheets in order — you'll finish in about 90 minutes.*

---

## How to use this workbook

Each worksheet pairs with a specific part of the playbook. Fill each one out by hand (print or PDF form fields) — the act of writing each number and decision forces clarity that reading doesn't. These worksheets become your operating documents. Stash them in a binder or a Google Drive folder and reference them quarterly.

**Order matters.** Worksheets 1–3 decide whether you should even run aged leads. 4–6 design your operation. 7–9 run it. 10 audits it. Don't skip ahead.
