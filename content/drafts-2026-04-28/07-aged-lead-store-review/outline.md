### H2: What they do well
- Pricing transparency. The single biggest competitive advantage. New buyers can comparison-shop without a sales call.
- No minimums. A solo agent can buy $50 worth of inventory to test the model. Most competitors require $500+ minimums.
- Self-service UX. Buy at 11 PM on a Tuesday without anyone in your inbox.
- Multi-vertical breadth. One account covers most agent and broker scenarios.
- Volume capacity. They have inventory at scale across all major verticals.
- Customer service responsiveness. Customer reviews consistently praise Troy and the support team. This is operationally important when you're trying to resolve a returns issue or a filtering question.
- Return policy. Up to 20% return credit is industry standard, but ALS honors it consistently. Some competitors are slower to credit.

### H2: What they don't do well
Be honest. This is what makes the review credible.

- **No real-time leads.** If you want to buy real-time inventory, ALS isn't your provider. You'll need a real-time vendor in addition.
- **No live transfers.** Same gap. Live-transfer buyers need a separate relationship.
- **No API delivery.** Lead delivery is via instant download or email. For high-volume operations that want lead-import automation, this is friction. (Workaround: use a Zapier or n8n flow to ingest the email-delivered CSV into your CRM.)
- **Limited compliance tooling.** TCPA documentation is available on request, but you don't get the kind of data-trail compliance evidence that LeadConduit-integrated providers offer at the high end.
- **Lead quality variance.** Like all aged inventory, ALS leads have a bad-data rate. Customer reports vary — some agents report the published bad-data rate (10-15%), others report higher (20-30%) on certain verticals or filters. The 20% return policy is the protection, but it requires you to track and submit returns systematically. Don't buy ALS inventory and not track returns.
- **No CRM integration.** Lead data has to be manually imported.

### H2: Aged Lead Store vs the alternatives — direct comparison
Brief honest comparison to four real competitors.

**vs The Leads Warehouse**: TLW has broader product mix (real-time + aged + live transfer + direct mail), but pricing is sales-quoted not published, and the platform is less self-service. ALS is faster to test and more transparent. TLW is better if you need multi-channel inventory under one roof.

**vs iLeads (CoreLogic)**: iLeads is a different product — credit-triggered data, premium pricing, enterprise-only. Not directly comparable for solo/small-team aged-internet-form buyers. iLeads makes sense for large mortgage shops with $10K+/month budgets.

**vs LeadPoint**: LeadPoint mixes aged with real-time and recommends combining them. Pricing is competitive but the platform is less self-service. ALS is cleaner for aged-only buyers.

**vs LeadsData / Aged Leads Depot**: These are smaller, value-tier competitors. Sometimes 10-20% cheaper on bulk orders, but data quality and return policy enforcement vary. ALS is the safer choice for buyers who value consistency.
